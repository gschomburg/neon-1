# NeoN+1 (Beta Runner): name ideas

A place to collect possible names for the game, kept out of the spec.

**Picked (2026-10-20): NeoN+1** (neon, with the N+1 ending), with a subtitle under it (working:
"RUNNER"). Beta Runner was the working title. Not in the game yet.

## Candidates

### The "N+1" family

From an earlier brainstorm (another chat). The idea: a word ending in N, written so the end reads
**N+1**. It ties into the game's core: each layer adds a node (layer *n* has *n + 1* nodes), so
there's always one more.

- **RoniN+1**: ronin, the masterless samurai; fits a lone runner / hacker.
- **XenoN+1**: xenon, the noble gas; "xeno" = foreign, alien.
- **SilicoN+1**: silicon, chips and circuits.
- **LogiN+1**: login; "cute" (the user). A hacking pun: one more login.
- **NeoN+1**: neon, the glowing gas of signs and cyberpunk cities. **Picked.**

## Favorites / shortlist

- **NeoN+1** (picked). Also liked: RoniN+1, XenoN+1, SilicoN+1, LogiN+1.
- Subtitle ideas: RUNNER (working), NET RUNNER, // RUNNER //, ONE MORE NODE, ICEBREAKER, JACK IN.

## Notes

- **N+1 styling to decide:** how it's written in text (`RONIN+1`, `RoniN+1`, `Ronin+1`), how it's
  said ("ronin plus one"?), and how the `+1` looks in the ASCII logo (e.g. the `+1` in the accent
  color, or in a small box).
- Things to check for a pick: does it fit the big title logo (title area is 32 x 20), does it work
  as a short tag, is the name already taken by another game, and is a domain / store name free.

## Title lockup samples (NeoN+1)

Five takes on a new title lockup: the current face (unchanged) plus background texture, with the
logotype tied into the art. Each fits the title area (**32 x 20**, the frame lines mark the edge).
In every one the **capitals are taller than the lowercase**: N and 1 are 5 rows, e and o are 3
rows, sitting on the same baseline. In game they'd keep the current colors (human half white,
circuit half cyan, logo in the accent color) and the eye blink / scan / glitch animations.

## Title lockup samples, round 2

Feedback on round 1: liked the noise in BLOCK and CIRCUIT, not the vertical lines in CIRCUIT;
prefer CIRCUIT's blocky letters; try overlapping letters, a sharper (metal) N, and a lowercase
first n.

- **Background (all five):** BLOCK's ramp static (`.:-=+*`) on the human side, thicker toward the
  top-left corner, and CIRCUIT's traces on the machine side, horizontal only (`o--+`, `+-o`).
- **Overlap:** a real overlap (letters sharing cells) breaks legibility fast: at one column the e and
  o melt together (`/ -_/ _ |`). So these use **tight kerning**: each letter slides as close as it
  can without two characters landing on one cell. They touch, they don't merge.
- **Metal N:** double stems with pointed tips (`/|` on top, `|/` and `\|` at the bottom) and a
  double diagonal in the middle. The 1 gets the same treatment.

## Title lockup samples, round 4: oldskool / Amiga styles (2026-10-22)

The user's references: the Amiga-style ASCII from roysac.com ("Styles of underground text art"): the BLUE SYSTEM banner (Roy/SAC) and Paso's ICE CREAM piece (Amiga and PC versions), plus the ascii-art.de collection for later. Much more expressive than our block letters; these ignore the 32-column title limit (most are 60-100 columns), the idea being the title could cycle between the face and a logotype. Rendered: `docs/art/logotype_round4.png`.
