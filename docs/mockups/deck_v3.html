THE DECK IN GAME

Greg's board v06 on the game's grid. The board scrolls in the middle; the bottom is the info area, like the market's. Tap anything: sockets, chips, and the parts that aren't sockets. Drag, scroll or use the arrow keys to move the board.

TRY THIS

  • Tap a socket. A full one shows its chip: group, rarity, power, what it does, where it came from. PULL or SWAP it.
  • Tap an empty socket (BT2, BI2, SH3...). The chips that fit are listed under it, two rows each. Tap one to see it, then SLOT it.
  • CHIPS (top) lists every chip you own, scrolling by row. Tap one, SLOT, and the sockets it fits blink on the board.
  • Power: the cell in PSU sets the budget (top right, and the ]8[ readout: power left). STOCK CELL is 6. Swap in LI-ION or REACTOR to run more.
  • The CRASH stick spells out the crash chip (DEADMAN scrolls; try the 1UP).
  • The BYTE: tap the bits on the board, then ENTER. Try 11111111, 00101010, 10110000, 01111111. The ticker under it answers.
  • The CPU: your hacker name (NAME cycles a few: long ones go on 2 lines), and the mk III upgrade.
  • The FUSE (the )( over the mk III board): install it for mk IV, then TEST: BREACH to see a boss blow it.
  • Also selectable: the ROM pins under the CPU (your BOARD posts; some are switches), the ports, the crew jacks, the uplink, the headers.
NOTES
1. One of each chip (Greg): a chip you already own, found again, scraps into its letters. The drop tables could skip chips you have, so finds stay new.
2. The art is 36 wide, the same as the game's grid. The game puts a scrollbar in the last column, so this mockup is one column wider. In the game: trim the art to 35, or drop the scrollbar and show ^ v in the margins.
3. The board is 56 rows and the view is 17: about 3.5 screens. A tap on a band's name could jump there.
