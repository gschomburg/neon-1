INFILTR8 VAULT FLOORS round 2

Five more maps for THE VAULT FLOOR, from Greg's notes on round 1. Each is the game's 36 x 23 phone grid. Every one has:

  • [⇄ PAD] / [⇄ GEAR] swaps the bottom 3 rows: the tray (gear, mods, ticker) or the controls (d-pad, wait, use). The tray rows hold the d-pad, so the map loses nothing.
  • Tap anything on the map (a camera, a door, a daemon, the stairs) and it lights up, and the info card (2 rows, over the controls) says what it is. With nothing tapped, the card is the message line.
  • In and out: you come in a door (⌂), and you leave by it, or by an alt exit (◘, ▒, ▾), each with a catch.
