WORKBENCH ROUND 3

Your notes on the keyboard version. Everything above the keyboard is on the game's 36-column grid; the keyboard is its own layer that slides up when you're on BUILD and drops away on the other tabs. Switch tabs to see the grid pages, and the cells decode in like the game's transitions.

BUILD

  • Big slots: each is 3 x 3 cells. A letter goes in at 3x size, cut into 9 cell pieces that fly up from the keyboard. Take one out and its pieces fall. Build and they rise into the terminal.
  • Pinned: your pins with each letter you have (its color) or are missing (red). Tap one to fill the slots.
  • Matches: as you type, the codes that start with your letters. That's what WARP and WORM were.
  • Special row: over the keyboard. A wildcard * fills any slot; the red G 0 D letters are G0D's own rarity. Try * R P, or spell G0D with the red ones.

SHRED · BOOK · LOCKER

  • SHRED is the market's sell page: a list, the details of the one you picked, and a S H R E D box. The letters you get back show on each row.
  • BOOK is a codex page: details on top (the 3 letters as big blocks, lit or missing), PIN and BUILD buttons, the list below. Secret words show as ???.
  • LOCKER: [ TAKE ] / [ STORE ] under the tabs, like the market's BUY / SELL. TAKE lists the locker; STORE lists your tray. Your tray sits at the bottom, drawn like the game's (mods, the belt, the info lines); tap one there to pick it. Storing a mod unequips it (still open: should that be allowed?).
