THE DECK IN GAME round 4

Greg's board v06 on the game's grid. The board scrolls in the middle, with the game's "more" rows; the bottom is a short card for what you picked. Tap a socket to pick its chip.

TRY THIS

  • Tap an empty socket (BT2, BI2, SH3...). The picker pops up: the chips that fit, or NONE. Tap one and it's in, and the picker closes.
  • Tap a full socket and the card under the board shows its chip. Tap it again (or CHANGE) for the picker.
  • Run out of power: put market in SH3 and ps in SH4. The newest chip goes grey with a red socket, and the warning stays at the bottom. Power goes to the oldest chips first. Swap the cell in PSU for LI-ION and they light up.
  • Tap [X] (top right) with a chip off: that's the warning you'd get leaving the deck.
  • SPOILS: its face shows the mode (RWD, HP+, $$$).
  • CHIPS (top): every chip you own, scrolling a chip at a time. Tap one, then SLOT, and the sockets it fits blink.
  • GLYPH (mk III, under the CPU upgrade) uses the same picker.
  • Still here from round 3: the BYTE (tap bits, ENTER; try 11111111), the CPU (NAME, mk III), the FUSE (install, TEST: BREACH), the ROM pins, the crew jacks, the ports, the uplink.

CHIP COLORS

Rarity (the game's chips), or your profile color. In your color the rarity moves to the socket's sides.

NOTES
1. The "more" rows take a row each, so the board shows 15 rows, and the scrollbar column is gone: the art's 36 columns fit the grid as is.
2. The game's scroll area steps a line at a time. A chip is 2 rows, so the picker and CHIPS step a whole chip, so you never see half of one. The board steps 5 rows a tap (it's 56 rows); drag or the wheel for fine moves.
3. The card dropped "from:" and "found again". They could live in the codex, if chips get a tab there.
