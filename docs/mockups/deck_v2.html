THE DECK ROUND 2

Less skill tree, more circuit board. A big CPU off to one side, modules scattered in loose clusters, and two widths of trace: thin signal lines, and wide power traces (double, on a copper strip) with 45-degree bends. The pulse starts at the CPU and runs out through every chip that's slotted; an empty socket stops it. Same controls as round 1: drag or scroll, tap a socket, slot / PULL / UPGRADE.

WHAT CHANGED

  • The CPU is a big square with pins on its edges, and the traces leave from the pins.
  • Wide traces are the power lines: a band of copper, two columns wide going down. Gold where they're the CPU's, the chip's color where they feed one.
  • Chains: a socket can be fed through another one. Pull GIV in E and the empty socket under it goes dark; slot something in the empty one and KIL's line lights.
  • Silkscreen: part numbers, a crystal, mounting holes, the board's name. Only decoration.
QUESTIONS
1. E or F, or more of these?
2. The chaining (a chip passes power on to the next socket): just a look, or a rule (an empty socket cuts off the ones past it)?
3. Names on the board, or only in the info area?
