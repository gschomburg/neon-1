THE DECK ROUND 1

Four ways to draw the deck as a circuit board. Each phone is live: drag or scroll the board, tap a socket and its info shows underneath, like the market. Tap a chip in an empty socket's list to slot it, PULL to take one out, UPGRADE to open the next tier. Cyan = shell chips, purple = rig chips, gold = the deck itself.

ALL FOUR

  • Full screen like the workbench: SAFEHOUSE // THE DECK on top, the board in the middle (17 rows, scrolls), the info area under it (11 rows).
  • Chips are outlined boxes, never filled tiles, so they don't read as the workbench's letters (those are filled with their rarity color).
  • Empty sockets are dashed. Locked ones are dotted, with the tier that opens them (mk3, mk4), and their wires are dotted too.
  • Wires between two live things (the deck, a slotted chip) carry a pulse.
  • From mk III on, the sockets take any chip. Below that, shell and rig sockets are separate.
