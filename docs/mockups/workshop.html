THE WORKBENCH

Three ways the safehouse workshop could look. Shred gear and mods into their letters, then spell a 3-letter short code to build one. The first screen in each row works: drag letters into the slots, tap an item to shred it, tap a recipe to fill it in. The other screens show that concept's popups and pages.

Recipes = short codes. WRP builds a WARP, SKP a SKIP, BUF a BUFFER. Every code is 3 letters.
Shred = an item's letters, plus a chance of one random bonus letter.
Letter rarity: E A R S P K W X Q Z 0 1 3 quest
No limit on banked letters (for now). The locker keeps 5 built things for the next run; gear stacks.
No wildcards. A word that's one letter off a recipe is a near miss: try it, and it might work.
Secret words show as ??? in the book. G0D needs the three quest letters.

A · CRAFTING TABLE

THE IDEA
The closest to Minecraft. Everything is on one screen in fixed boxes: 3 slots and an output at the top, your bag next to the shredder, every letter you own in a grid, and the locker along the bottom.
GOOD
  • Everything at a glance; nothing hidden.
  • The letter grid shows what's missing (empty tiles).
  • Easy to build: plain boxes.
WATCH
  • Tight on a phone; the letter grid gets long.
  • Feels more like a menu than a place.

B · TERMINAL

THE IDEA
You're a hacker, so you type it. Your letter bank is a keyboard: each key shows how many of that letter you have, and keys you don't have are dead. The prompt autocompletes recipes as you go, like a shell. The book is a man page.
GOOD
  • Very NeoN+1; typing seeds already works like this.
  • Autocomplete teaches recipes without a book.
  • Fits a phone: a keyboard is a phone thing.
WATCH
  • Less dragging, less Minecraft.
  • Shredding lives on another tab.

C · THE BENCH

THE IDEA
A real tabletop. Letter tiles lie loose on the bench, stacked in piles, and you push them around wherever you want. Drop an item in the shredder and its letters land on the table. The jig has 3 slots and a PRESS. Pin recipes to the wall like a shopping list. The bag, locker and blueprints are in a drawer.
GOOD
  • The most physical and toy-like: drag everything.
  • Pinned recipes show the letters you're missing.
  • Room for a big G0D moment.
WATCH
  • A messy table with lots of letters.
  • The most work to build.
