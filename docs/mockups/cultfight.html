THE CULT FIGHT round 1

Five takes on the G0D.exe quest's boss fight, on the game's 36 x 23 phone grid. The pieces are the same in each: the keyboard at the bottom, the cultists with their words, the cult leader and the altar. There is no header: INT is pips on the spacebar, and the leader's bars sit by his face. The draft is in docs/QUESTS.md ("The G0D.exe quest: the cult"). The cult and its leader have no names yet: THE PRIOR is a placeholder.

MONTY a word G0D.exe knows (its red) MONTY G0D.exe typing it GLACIER? a new word: yours GLACIER you typing it K a key you're out of (1 INT to press) C the key you just pressed G a letter on the altar
