# Beta Runner: ASCII art drop-off

Put art here and I'll pull it into the game. One code block per piece, under a heading with its name.

- **Title area:** 32 wide x 20 tall (the face + BETA RUNNER logo together).
- **Corp logos:** up to 24 wide x 12 tall. Smaller is fine; it gets centered.
- **Hacker logos** (player profiles, v10): up to 22 wide x 5 tall. They share the CORP BREACHED screen with the mod offers, so 5 rows is the limit. 15 are in the game (`HACKER_LOGOS`): SKULL, ANON, TROLL, LOLCAT, GLIDER, SUDO, ACID, DOGE, BOT, JOLLY ROGER, 404, ROFLCOPTER, THIS IS FINE, THE EYE, HACK THE PLANET. To redraw one or add one, paste it under `## HACKER: name`, plus a 3-character mark for small spots (like `{x}`).
- Even widths center exactly on the 36-column screen; odd widths sit half a character left (fine for art with one center column).
- Paste over the blank frame (the `|` and `+` lines mark the edge; I strip them). Spaces only, no tabs. Backslashes are fine inside code blocks.
- Optional: a note under the block for color (which parts are which color, what animates).
