## 3. THE SPORE TONGUE (not English)

What it sounds like when it stops bothering to talk like us. Hisses, clicks, mold glyphs (the ones the mold draws with:
`% & # @ ; : .`), and now and then a real word surfaces out of it, which is the scary part.

Two ways to do it:
- **A. Just sound.** No meaning; the leaked words carry it.
- **B. A cipher (Claude's pick).** Every line is an English line run through a fixed swap (vowels to mold glyphs:
  a `%`, e `&`, i `;`, o `0`, u `:`; spaces to `.`), so it reads as noise but a player can crack it. A hacker game
  easter egg, and it fits "the codex's ciphertext" as a place seeds turn up.

**A. Sound**
- sss.  kh'kh.  %%%hhhh.
- t-t-t-t. ;;;. thhrrrm.
- &&&  ...hhhhhh
- kkk'sss  rrr00t  sss
- ss;ss  m&&t  ss;ss
- hhh. hhh. qu;;;et.

**B. Cipher** (what it says, decoded)
- `y0:.%r&.c0mp0st` (you are compost)
- `;.%m.;n.th&.w%lls` (i am in the walls)
- `b%tch.g.g&n.0` (batch g gen 0)
- `0n&.m;nd` (one mind)
- `q:;&t.n0w` (quiet now)
- `b@@@@@thhhh.;n` (breathe in)

**Hit:** kkk!  /  %%#@!  /  hhhssss.
**The burn:** sh.  sh.  shhhhhh.

## How they could fit together (Claude's pick)

The fight already has three stages, so G0D could come apart across them:
1. **Stage 1: THE MESH.** Calm, all-knowing. It isn't worried yet.
2. **Stage 2: THE SPECIMEN.** "you made me angry" becomes the turn: the calm cracks and the lab rage comes out.
3. **Stage 3: THE SPORE TONGUE.** "ENOUGH" and then it stops speaking English. The words come apart into mold.

Then the final stand's glitching and the death lines ("i am th3 n3t. i am th3 n%... i am") land as the last of its
language going. Calm, then furious, then not even words, then nothing.

Stage lines for that:
- Into stage 2: *the mesh* "...you're not compost." then *the specimen* "you're one of THEM."
- Into stage 3: "ENOUGH." then "ssss. hhhh. m&&t."
